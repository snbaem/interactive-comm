# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. By taking part in this project (issues, pull requests,
discussions or any other project space) you agree to follow it.

## In short

- Be respectful and welcoming. Assume good intent.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, insults, sexualized language or imagery, or personal attacks.
- Do not publish other people's private information, and do not post real
  customer or personal data in issues, examples or test files.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the
project maintainers through
[GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/snbaem/interactive-comm/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting a maintainer listed on the repository. Maintainers will
review every report, respond as quickly as they can, and keep the reporter's
identity confidential.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that do not follow this code of conduct, and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they consider
harmful.
